# V0.6.5 theme report

Forest is the default. Sunset unlocks from validated persisted Classic best score ≥ 500; Snow unlocks after ten distinct Challenge completions. ThemeStore maintains a separate versioned selection/unlock save and falls back to Forest on a fresh install; unavailable or corrupt evidence is not interpreted as an unlock. A locked theme cannot be applied. The menu screen shows background previews, conditions and locked/unlocked/selected state. Selected resources update the main menu, game and relevant menu screens; no collision, physics or scoring parameters were changed.

Godot integration [45830006201](https://github.com/sandikovcusu-commits/MergeAnimal/actions/runs/45830006201): 20 checks plus a separate-process restore. The genuine Classic scoring run [45830005801](https://github.com/sandikovcusu-commits/MergeAnimal/actions/runs/45830005801) generated score from physical collisions using a forced dog queue and unlocked Sunset; the Challenge run [45830005738](https://github.com/sandikovcusu-commits/MergeAnimal/actions/runs/45830005738) checked nine versus ten distinct completions, including fixture-assisted challenges. Repeats do not add distinct completions. Selection/save corruption and old-save paths are also tested using fixtures, not claimed as natural play.

See `art_reports/v0650_themes_locales/`: X11 scripted fixture screenshots of all three themes at 360×640, 720×1280, 1366×768. Desktop intentionally uses portrait letterboxing. They are not mobile screenshots or human playtests. Buttons shrink to ~30 physical px high at 360×640; touch ergonomics are not yet accepted.
