# V0.7.0 animation and game feel

All 12 existing species share `AnimalVisual.gd`: gentle idle breathing, randomized 3–8 second individual timing and 0.12–0.18 second blink cue, plus drop, landing and merge scale tweens. Only the child visual scales; body transforms, collision radii, mass, collision contact and impulses are unchanged. The merge manager still commits one real collision and creates one result, then animates that result without re-triggering gameplay.

**Limitation:** the 12 existing source images have no independent eyes or expression layers. The blink is a small whole-sprite squash cue, NOT an actual eye closure. Happy/surprise select an existing expression sprite if supplied, otherwise use subtle shape motion and the original image. No 24 expression textures were created, and the previous release-acceptance gate still correctly reports them missing. Actual eye, face and 12 individually art-directed animations need new licensed layered art and design review.

Merge adds bounded procedural sparkle, burst, leaves, combo highlight, a floating score and size-based audio. Golden adds glow, sparkles, score, existing localized Golden label and a short camera offset; Reduced Effects disables the camera shake and extra decorative particles, not essential score/status information. Up to 40 effects and 8 floating score labels are active; effects delete themselves. UI uses a short non-blocking scene fade, visual-only button compression, result overlay fade and existing notification animation. Buttons never dispatch a second action through animation.

Godot engine tests [45838054585](https://github.com/sandikovcusu-commits/MergeAnimal/actions/runs/45838054585) passed 91 checks and one separate-process read, including every tier's drop/landing/merge visual scale vs unchanged collision radius, randomized blink-cue bounds, real Classic merge/one score, real Golden Garden collision, Golden sound, reduced camera motion and no duplicate Golden score. Challenge access is seeded for Golden; the challenge's configured board and collisions are real. These checks do not prove aesthetic quality or actual eyelid movement.

X11 720×1280 screenshots: `art_reports/v070_game_feel/`. The merge screenshot and five-second silent `scripted_merge.mp4` use real physics with deterministic preview selections, not a human playtest. The clip is H.264/yuv420p, 360×640 at 15 fps; it does not capture or validate audio.
