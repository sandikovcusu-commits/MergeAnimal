# V0.6.5 localization report

English, Azerbaijani, Turkish and Russian are registered through Godot TranslationServer, with settings persistence and live refresh. Achievement titles/descriptions and animal names on gameplay/notification UI use translated keys; existing dictionaries include Challenge goals, animal descriptions, tutorial, menus, settings and power-up labels. Bundled DejaVu Sans (license alongside the TTF) supplies Azerbaijani Ə/ə and Cyrillic, including the custom drawn gameplay/menu labels.

Godot CI [45831030136](https://github.com/sandikovcusu-commits/MergeAnimal/actions/runs/45831030136) reports 74 locale checks, no failures; each English dictionary key exists and is non-empty in each locale. Tests check all 15 Achievement names and descriptions per locale, missing keys, glyph support, persistence and menu/paused-game live language changes. Key coverage does not prove every possible dynamically formatted string is idiomatic or visually fits; a native-speaker review and exhaustive interactive traversal remain open.

X11 fixture screenshots cover three themes × four locale menus and localized theme/achievement/challenge/settings screens at 360×640, 720×1280 and 1366×768. Reviewed samples show Azerbaijani and Russian glyphs and readable wrapped labels; scroll lists intentionally clip offscreen items at their viewport boundary. No full traversal or objective accessibility/contrast measurement was performed. Some 360 px text and ~30 px controls are small for touch; physical device and safe-area review is required. Screenshots do not constitute human play.
